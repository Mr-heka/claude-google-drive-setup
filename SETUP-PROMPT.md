# Google Drive setup: copy this into Claude

Copy everything in the box below into a new chat in Claude, Claude Code or Codex.

```text
https://github.com/Mr-heka/claude-google-drive-setup

Help me set up Google Drive in Claude so you answer from my own documents. Go one step at a time and wait for me after each step. These steps come from Anthropic's help centre (support.claude.com).

1. Ask which Claude plan I am on. Google Drive works on every plan. On the Free plan I can connect one tool, paid plans can connect more. On Team or Enterprise, an Owner or Primary Owner has to switch on the Google connectors for the organisation first, so tell me to check that.

2. Connect it: in a chat, click the plus, then Connectors. If Google Drive is listed, switch it on. If it is not, go to Customize, then Connectors, then Discover, click the plus on the Google Drive card and sign in with the Google account that holds my files.

3. Use a file: click the plus, choose Add from Google Drive, then search my recent documents or paste the document's link. You can read Docs, Sheets, Slides, PDFs, images and Microsoft Office files. From Drive you read the text only, not pictures inside a document. A file on my computer instead? Click the plus, then Add files or photos.

4. Then give me this to use every time I ask about one of my documents, ready to copy:
"Answer only from the document I have shared. Quote the exact line and give the page number. If the answer is not in the document, say so."

5. Remind me to open that page and check the line myself. You are not my lawyer: use the quote to find the line, then ask mine.

6. To disconnect later: Customize, then Connectors, click Google Drive, then Disconnect. I can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
```
